Most urgent actions

GARD — the local campaign group — says these are the most important things you can do right now.

  • Contribute to the GARD crowdfunder

    Help fund proper scrutiny of Thames Water's DCO application. GARD needs funds for legal and flood expertise during the examination.

    Take action

  • Sign the GARD/38 Degrees petition

    Add your name to the joint GARD and CPRE Oxfordshire petition calling for a stop to Thames Water's plans for the White Horse Reservoir. 8,000+ have already signed.

    Take action

  • Take the joint parish survey

    Complete the joint survey across eight affected parishes to record local impacts for use in councils' Local Impact Reports and consultation responses.

    Take action

  • Write to the Secretary of State for the Environment

    Contact Emma Reynolds MP, Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, to raise concerns and request an independent cost re-evaluation comparing the reservoir to the Severn Thames Transfer alternative before any DCO is granted.
