Cearn Solar Farm: What You Can Do

The Vale of White Horse is a statutory consultee for this application, not the decision-maker. Write to your Vale councillors asking them to strengthen the Vale's Local Impact Report -- not to block the scheme. The decision is made by the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ), not Defra.

Act now

Ask your parish and Vale councillor to shape the Environmental Statement

The public cannot respond to the Planning Inspectorate directly during scoping. Ask your parish clerk and Vale ward councillor to press for specific items in the Environmental Statement: field-level land grades, viewpoints from village conservation areas, HGV routes avoiding villages, cumulative assessment with the reservoir construction programme, and battery storage siting and fire safety.

Who can do this: Anyone, via parish and Vale

Deadline: 2026-11-23

Respond to the developer's Stage 2 consultation, the main chance to shape the layout and cable route. It is no longer a legal requirement but the developer says it will run it. Expected February to March 2027.

Who can do this: Anyone

After DCO acceptance

Register as an Interested Party

Register as an Interested Party with a Relevant Representation on the Planning Inspectorate project page within the advertised window after the application is accepted. Only Interested Parties can take full part in the examination.

Who can do this: Anyone

Planning Inspectorate: Cearn Solar Farm (EN0110044)

Lobby for strong Local Impact Reports and take part in the examination

Lobby the Vale and Oxfordshire County Council for strong Local Impact Reports. As an Interested Party, make written submissions and speak at open floor hearings during the examination.

Who can do this: Interested Parties

Planning Inspectorate: Cearn Solar Farm (EN0110044)

Judicial review

Judicial review in the High Court under section 118 of the Planning Act 2008 is available on legal and procedural grounds only. It must be brought within 6 weeks of the decision. This is general information, not legal advice.

Who can do this: Anyone with standing

Ongoing

Write to your MPs, councillors and the DESNZ Secretary of State

Write to your MPs and councillors (see representatives) and to the DESNZ Secretary of State by post. Focus on planning matters: landscape, heritage setting, farmland grades, flood risk, traffic, noise, ecology, battery safety, and cumulative effects.

Who can do this: Anyone
