Facts about the White Horse Reservoir project

Timeline

Events in reverse chronological order.

Future milestones (2040 and beyond)
  1. 2040
    First water available. Main works complete approximately 2043. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  1. 2029
    Main construction start (Thames Water website figure). Note: the PEI Report and October 2026 press coverage give 2032 as the main works start date. Both figures are from Thames Water. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  2. January 2027
    RAPID gate four submission planned. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
    You can: Monitor Thames Water's project page for DCO submission
  3. November 2026
    DCO application submission expected. Not submitted as at 1 November 2026. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
    You can: Monitor Thames Water's project page for DCO submission, Submit a Relevant Representation
  4. 12 October 2026
    GARD reports that Oxfordshire County Council's submission to the targeted consultation said it 'fails to meet three of the four Gunning Principles' and called for the DCO submission to be delayed. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  5. 1 October 2026
    Three joint ventures shortlisted for the main works contract. Bids expected spring 2027, contract award autumn 2027. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  6. 15 September 2026
    White Horse Reservoir Growth Coalition launched at a parliamentary event. Led by BusinessLDN and Thames Valley Chamber of Commerce, with eleven founding members. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  7. 9 September 2026
    Targeted consultation on access points opens (runs to 7 October 2026). Access points raised from 6 to 17. Thames Water acknowledges 'overall construction traffic movements will be higher than previously forecast'. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  8. July 2026
    Pre-application Programme Document published by Thames Water. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  9. June 2026
    Thames Water publishes 'community research' findings: 2,004 adults surveyed, 89% said the reservoir is 'important'. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  10. 14 May 2026
    RAPID confirms gate three passed, clearing the project to proceed towards planning. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  11. April 2026
    Clay compaction trial summary published by Thames Water. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  12. 19 March 2026
    Secretary of State issues a section 35 direction letter, published on the Planning Inspectorate's site. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  13. 13 January 2026
    Oxfordshire County Council statutory consultation response submitted; the council says it 'cannot support the application in its current form due to the significant environmental impacts'. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  14. January 2026
    Project renamed White Horse Reservoir. £5.7bn main works contractor tender issued on Find a Tender (notice 005547-2026). Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  15. 3 December 2025
    RAPID gate three draft decision published; consultation period opens to 29 January 2026. Decision unlocks up to £100m for further development. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  16. 28 October 2025
    Statutory consultation (sections 42/47) opens with the Preliminary Environmental Information (PEI) Report. Nine events held, more than 1,200 attendees and about 2,500 responses by its close on 13 January 2026. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  17. 29 January 2025
    Chancellor Rachel Reeves lists White Horse Reservoir among 'nine new reservoirs' backed by the government, alongside £7.9bn of water resources investment. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  18. September 2024
    Government approves Thames Water's Water Resources Management Plan 2024 (WRMP24), including the White Horse Reservoir. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  19. 8 October 2024
    Planning Inspectorate adopts the EIA Scoping Opinion. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  20. 28 August 2024
    EIA scoping request submitted to the Planning Inspectorate. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  21. June 2024
    Non-statutory consultation opens on interim masterplan and access road options (runs to August 2024). Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  22. January 2022
    RAPID gate one final decision published. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  23. 2019
    RAPID (Regulators' Alliance for Progressing Infrastructure Development) gated process for strategic resource options begins, including the South East Strategic Reservoir Option (SESRO). Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  24. 2011
    Government rejects the £1bn reservoir following the public inquiry, concluding 'need not proven'. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  25. 2010
    Public inquiry begins into Thames Water's Water Resources Management Plan, examining the Upper Thames Reservoir proposal. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
  26. 2006
    Thames Water proposes the Upper Thames Reservoir near Abingdon, described by BBC as 'biggest in 25 years'. Source
    Last checked: 2026-11-01
