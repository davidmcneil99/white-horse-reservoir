About Vale Watch

Vale Watch is an independent community resource covering two nationally significant infrastructure proposals affecting the Vale of White Horse: the White Horse Reservoir (Thames Water, DCO reference WA010005) and the Cearn Solar Farm (Telis Energy UK, DCO reference EN0110044). The site collects and presents publicly available information from Planning Inspectorate records, applicant documents, and council sources to help local residents engage with both applications.

Vale Watch is not affiliated with any campaign group. GARD (Group Against Reservoir Development) is the principal community opposition group for the reservoir project; Protect the Vale is the equivalent group for the solar farm. Both are independent organisations and neither runs this site. Information is presented factually; the site does not advocate on behalf of either campaign.

Data is sourced from published application documents, Planning Inspectorate records, council minutes, and press releases. Last checked: 2026-11-03. Sources are linked from each item. To report an error, open a GitHub issue: Report an error.

White Horse Reservoir (WA010005)

Thames Water's proposed reservoir near Abingdon would be the largest in England. The DCO application is with the Planning Inspectorate. GARD (Group Against Reservoir Development) is the principal local campaign group: groupagainstreservoirdevelopment.org.

Cearn Solar Farm (EN0110044)

Telis Energy UK's proposed solar farm would cover around 2,800 acres (approximately 1,130 hectares) of agricultural land in the Vale. The scoping stage is under way with the Planning Inspectorate. Protect the Vale is the local campaign group.
