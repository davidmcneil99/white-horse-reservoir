About this site

Who runs this site

This site is run by volunteers who are concerned about the proposed White Horse Reservoir (SESRO) project. It is independent of GARD (Group Against Reservoir Development) and is not the official GARD website.

The site collects and presents publicly available information about the Thames Water White Horse Reservoir Development Consent Order (DCO) application. It does not represent the views of any particular organisation.

Data sources

Data is sourced from Thames Water's published application documents, Planning Inspectorate records, council minutes, and press releases. Last checked: 2026-11-02.

Sources are linked from each item. Where a correction has been made, it is noted on the relevant item and listed on the corrections page.
