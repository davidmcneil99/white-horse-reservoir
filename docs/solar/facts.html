Cearn Solar Farm (EN0110044)

A Nationally Significant Infrastructure Project (NSIP) proposed south of Garford in the Vale of White Horse. See also the Combined Concerns page for how this scheme interacts with the reservoir proposal.

Scheme summary

Applicant Cearn Solar Farm Limited (wholly owned subsidiary of Telis Energy UK)
Size Up to 500 MW, approximately 2,800 acres
Location South of Garford, east of Charney Bassett, north of West Hanney, south of Goosey, north-east of Baulking (Vale of White Horse district)
Decision-maker Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ)
Planning Inspectorate reference EN0110044

The Vale of White Horse is a statutory consultee, not the planning authority for this application.

Positions and statements

Statements from public figures, organisations and bodies about the Cearn Solar Farm proposal.

Motion passed with amendments: solar should go on non-agricultural land first and avoid high-grade farmland; local shared ownership or benefit schemes and biodiversity gain; the Vale should not bear NSIP harms while benefits leave the district; cumulative impact with Botley West, the West Oxfordshire Energy Park and the reservoir must be considered.

Vale of White Horse District Council, Statutory consultee; local planning authority 21 October 2026 Source

Note: The motion asks for a letter to Defra, but the decision-maker for Cearn is the DESNZ Secretary of State.

Written answers: Cearn is decided by the Secretary of State; the council is working with the Oxfordshire Association of Local Councils so parishes can input; officers are taking a programme-wide approach to cumulative impacts through the Joint Local Plan.
