Cearn Solar Farm (EN0110044)

Telis Energy UK proposes a 500 MW solar and battery storage scheme covering around 2,800 acres near Garford, Charney Bassett, West Hanney, Goosey and Baulking. This is a nationally significant infrastructure project (EN0110044). The Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero decides.

Most urgent actions

These are the most time-sensitive actions right now. The Vale's scoping response is due 23 November 2026.

  1. Ask your parish and Vale councillor to shape the Environmental Statement

    The public cannot respond to the Planning Inspectorate directly during scoping. Ask your parish clerk and Vale ward councillor to press for specific items in the Environmental Statement: field-level land grades, viewpoints from village conservation areas, HGV routes avoiding villages, cumulative assessment with the reservoir construction programme, and battery storage siting and fire safety.

    Take action

  2. Register for Planning Inspectorate and developer updates

    Register for Planning Inspectorate email updates on EN0110044 and the developer's updates. You can also write to the developer at info@cearnsolarfarm.co.uk or by post: Freepost CEARN SOLAR FARM.

    Take action

  3. Join Protect the Vale and ask your parish to engage

    Join Protect the Vale's update list. Ask your parish council or meeting to engage with the Vale's Oxfordshire Association of Local Councils route for parish input.

    Take action
