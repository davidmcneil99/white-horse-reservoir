What you can do

There are a number of ways you can engage with the planning process for the White Horse Reservoir (SESRO) project.

Act now

Sign up for Planning Inspectorate email updates

Register on the WA010005 project page to receive updates directly from the Planning Inspectorate on application status, acceptance decisions and examination dates.

Who can do this: Anyone

More information

Sign the GARD/38 Degrees petition

Add your name to the joint GARD and CPRE Oxfordshire petition calling for a stop to Thames Water's plans for the White Horse Reservoir.

Who can do this: Anyone

More information

Join GARD (Group Against Reservoir Development)

Join the local campaign group for updates, newsletters, and to support the organisation's work funding DCO expertise.

Who can do this: Anyone

More information

Contribute to the GARD crowdfunder

Help fund GARD's DCO expertise by donating to their crowdfunder campaign. Reached £5k in its first week after launching in October 2026.

Who can do this: Anyone

More information

Take the joint parish survey

Complete the joint survey across eight affected parishes to record local impacts for use in councils' Local Impact Reports and consultation responses.

Who can do this: Residents of affected parishes

More information

Write to Thames Water

Contact the project team directly at whitehorsereservoir@thameswater.co.uk to raise concerns before the DCO is submitted. Thames Water is required to consider pre-application responses.

Who can do this: Anyone

More information

Write to your MP

Contact your Member of Parliament to raise concerns about the reservoir, construction traffic impacts, and the adequacy of consultation. MPs can table questions, request debates and write formally to ministers.

Who can do this: Constituents

More information

Write to your county councillor

Contact your Oxfordshire County Councillor to ask them to raise highway, traffic and environmental concerns. Oxfordshire County Council is a statutory consultee and the highway authority.

Who can do this: Oxfordshire residents

More information

Write to your district councillor

Contact your Vale of White Horse or South Oxfordshire District Councillor to raise concerns. The Vale Leader has already written to Defra demanding intervention.

Who can do this: Vale of White Horse and South Oxfordshire residents

More information

Speak at a parish council meeting

Attend your parish council meeting and use the public participation slot to raise the reservoir on the record. Parish councils' Local Impact Reports carry weight in the DCO examination.

Who can do this: Residents in affected parishes

More information

Ask your parish clerk about missing meeting minutes

Under the Transparency Code for Smaller Authorities and the Freedom of Information Act 2000, parish councils must publish minutes within one month of a meeting and respond to FOI requests within 20 working days.

Who can do this: Anyone

More information

This is general information, not legal advice.

Submit an EIR request to Thames Water

Under the Environmental Information Regulations 2004, Thames Water counts as a public authority for EIR purposes and must respond within 20 working days. Use this to request documents about traffic modelling, access point assessments, or environmental mitigation.

Who can do this: Anyone

More information

This is general information, not legal advice.

Submit an FOI request to Oxfordshire County Council or Vale of White Horse DC

Under the Freedom of Information Act 2000, local authorities must respond within 20 working days. Use this to request correspondence with Thames Water, consultation responses, or highway assessment documents.

Who can do this: Anyone

More information

This is general information, not legal advice.

Ongoing

Monitor Thames Water's project page for DCO submission

The DCO application is expected in November 2026. Check the Thames Water project page and the Planning Inspectorate's WA010005 project page for confirmation of submission, and watch for the acceptance decision (within 28 days of submission).

Who can do this: Anyone

More information

Follow GARD for campaign updates and newsletters

GARD publishes regular newsletters, distributed through parish councils and on their website. Sign up to stay informed about consultation dates, examination milestones and campaign events.

Who can do this: Anyone
