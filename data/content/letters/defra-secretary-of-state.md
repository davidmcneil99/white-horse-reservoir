I am writing to you as a resident of {{sender_area}}, in {{sender_constituency}}, regarding the proposed White Horse Reservoir (Planning Inspectorate reference WA010005). I am deeply concerned about the project's escalating costs, the adequacy of the consultation process, and the impact on my community.

The estimated cost of the project has risen from £2.2bn to £7.5bn. This dramatic escalation significantly undermines the business case and risks leaving bill payers and the public purse liable. I urge you to commission an independent review of the project's value for money and to require a genuine reassessment of alternatives, including the Severn-Thames Transfer scheme, before the Development Consent Order application is determined.

Oxfordshire County Council has stated, in its statutory consultation response, that it cannot support the application in its current form due to significant environmental impacts. The council has also said that the targeted consultation on access points fails to meet three of the four Gunning Principles, and has called for the DCO submission to be delayed. I share these concerns. The consultation process has not given local residents a proper opportunity to engage with the proposals.

Construction of the reservoir would last more than ten years and would bring large numbers of heavy goods vehicles through villages across the area, including {{sender_area}}. The number of access points has risen from six to seventeen in September 2026 alone. The impacts on road safety, air quality, and quality of life are severe and have not been properly assessed. I ask you to ensure that no DCO application is accepted until these concerns have been addressed and a further round of consultation has been held.

I would welcome a response setting out the steps your department intends to take.
