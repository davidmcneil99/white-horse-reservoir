CONSTRUCTION TRAFFIC

The number of construction access points increased from six to seventeen in September 2026. Thames Water has acknowledged that overall construction traffic movements will be higher than previously forecast. This increase will directly affect roads in and near {{sender_area}}. I ask the Examining Authority to scrutinise closely:

- the traffic modelling for each access point, and whether it reflects the full increase from six to seventeen points;
- the proposed Code of Construction Practice, and whether it contains binding routing restrictions and HGV limits;
- the cumulative impact on roads in {{sender_area}}.

CONSULTATION ADEQUACY

Oxfordshire County Council has stated that the targeted consultation on access points failed to meet three of the four Gunning Principles. I am concerned that the Environmental Statement may not adequately address the impacts identified by local residents and councils during that consultation.
