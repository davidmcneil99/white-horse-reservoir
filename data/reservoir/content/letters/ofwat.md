I am writing to you as a resident of {{sender_area}}, in {{sender_constituency}}, regarding the proposed White Horse Reservoir and the RAPID gated process (Planning Inspectorate reference WA010005).

I understand that RAPID gate three was confirmed in May 2026, and that gate four is expected in January 2027. I am writing to ask Ofwat to use the RAPID process to require a more thorough assessment of value for money and of alternatives before the project progresses further.

The estimated cost of the project has risen from £2.2bn to £7.5bn. This is a more than threefold increase, and it significantly weakens the case for the scheme compared with alternatives such as the Severn-Thames Transfer, water efficiency investment, and smaller distributed storage options. I note that the Water Resources South East regional plan selected the reservoir but that RAPID required the three companies to update their best value assessment through the WRMP29 cycle. I ask that Ofwat makes the outcome of that assessment publicly available before gate four is confirmed.

I also ask Ofwat to clarify how bill payers across the three companies' areas will be protected if the project's costs continue to rise, and how it will ensure that the separately licensed Infrastructure Provider model does not insulate Thames Water from accountability for the scheme's impact on customers.

I would welcome a response setting out Ofwat's position on these points.
