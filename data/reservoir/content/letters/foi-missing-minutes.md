**Legal basis**

This request is made on the following grounds:

1. **Transparency Code for Smaller Authorities (2014):** parish councils and parish meetings with annual turnover up to £25,000 must publish draft minutes of formal meetings within one month of the meeting, free of charge and in an accessible format online.

2. **Freedom of Information Act 2000:** I am entitled to request recorded information held by the authority. The authority must respond within 20 working days of receiving this request. The authority's publication scheme (normally the ICO model publication scheme) covers minutes of formal meetings.

3. **Environmental Information Regulations 2004 (EIR):** where the minutes contain information relating to environmental matters (including discussion of the White Horse Reservoir or other planning and infrastructure matters), they fall within the scope of the EIR. Requests under the EIR may be made verbally or in writing, and the authority must respond within 20 working days.

**This is general information, not legal advice.** If the authority refuses to provide the minutes or fails to respond within the statutory time limit, you may escalate to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Please confirm receipt of this request. I ask that you treat it as a request under the Freedom of Information Act 2000 and/or the Environmental Information Regulations 2004, as appropriate, and provide the minutes within 20 working days.
