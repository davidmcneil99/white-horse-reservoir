I am writing to you as a resident of {{sender_area}}, in {{sender_constituency}}, regarding the proposed White Horse Reservoir (Planning Inspectorate reference WA010005).

I am writing before the Development Consent Order application is submitted to set out my concerns and to ask Thames Water to take specific steps to address them.

The increase in access points from six to seventeen in September 2026 has come as a shock to many affected communities. Thames Water has acknowledged that overall construction traffic will be higher than previously forecast, yet the targeted consultation that followed was criticised by Oxfordshire County Council for failing to meet three of the four Gunning Principles. Residents in {{sender_area}} have had insufficient opportunity to understand, comment on, and propose mitigations for the impacts of the additional access points on their daily lives.

I ask Thames Water to:

1. Delay the DCO submission until a full and compliant further consultation on access points has been carried out.
2. Publish detailed traffic modelling for each of the seventeen access points, showing peak HGV movements per hour on every affected road.
3. Commit to binding routing restrictions, daily HGV limits, and out-of-hours restrictions as part of the Code of Construction Practice.
4. Establish a residents' community liaison group, separate from the Technical Liaison Group, so that those living near construction routes have a direct voice throughout the build.

I look forward to receiving a substantive response.
