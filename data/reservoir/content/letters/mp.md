I am writing to you as one of your constituents, living in {{sender_area}}, {{sender_constituency}}, regarding the proposed White Horse Reservoir (Planning Inspectorate reference WA010005).

Thames Water expects to submit a Development Consent Order (DCO) application for a 150-billion-litre reservoir south-west of Abingdon in November 2026. This would be a nationally significant infrastructure project affecting my community for more than a decade of construction. The number of construction access points has risen from six to seventeen in September 2026 alone, and Thames Water has admitted that overall construction traffic will be higher than previously forecast.

I am concerned about the following:

1. The cost of the scheme has risen from £2.2bn to £7.5bn, significantly undermining the business case.
2. Oxfordshire County Council has said the targeted consultation fails to meet three of the four Gunning Principles and has called for the DCO submission to be delayed.
3. There has been no adequate assessment of alternatives, including the Severn-Thames Transfer scheme.
4. Construction traffic would pass through {{sender_area}} and neighbouring villages for more than a decade.

I would be grateful if you could raise these concerns with the Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, support calls for an independent review of the project's value for money, and press for a further round of public consultation before any DCO application is accepted.

Please let me know what steps you are taking on this issue.
