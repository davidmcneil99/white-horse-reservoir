Local government reorganisation

  • Decision: On 16 Jul 2026 the government chose three unitary councils for Oxfordshire and West Berkshire, subject to Parliament approving a Structural Changes Order
  • New councils: North Oxfordshire Council; Greater Oxford Council (Oxford plus nine Vale parishes: Appleton-with-Eaton, Besselsleigh, Cumnor, Kennington, North Hinksey, South Hinksey, Sunningwell, Wootton, Wytham); Ridgeway Council (West Berkshire plus the rest of South Oxfordshire and the Vale)
  • Affected area: All reservoir-affected places, including Abingdon and the Hanneys, are outside the nine parishes and so fall in Ridgeway Council (by inference)
  • Timetable: Structural Changes Order expected autumn 2026 (not confirmed laid); elections to new councils May 2027 (shadow authorities); vesting day 1 Apr 2028
  • Abolished 1 apr 2028: Oxfordshire County Council, Vale of White Horse, South Oxfordshire, Oxford City, Cherwell, West Oxfordshire, West Berkshire
  • Parishes: Continue unchanged
  • App impact: The DCO examination (likely 2027) will run while the current councils are still in place, but the decision and construction will fall under Ridgeway Council. Plan for new ModernGov hosts and councillors from May 2027
