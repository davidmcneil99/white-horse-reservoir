Cearn Solar Farm (EN0110044)

A Nationally Significant Infrastructure Project (NSIP) proposed south of Garford in the Vale of White Horse. See also the Combined Impacts page for how this scheme interacts with the reservoir proposal.

Scheme summary

Applicant Cearn Solar Farm Limited (wholly owned subsidiary of Telis Energy UK)
Size Up to 500 MW, approximately 2,800 acres
Location South of Garford, east of Charney Bassett, north of West Hanney, south of Goosey, north-east of Baulking (Vale of White Horse district)
Decision-maker Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ)
Planning Inspectorate reference EN0110044

The Vale of White Horse is a statutory consultee, not the planning authority for this application.
